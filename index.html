02 / RESEARCH

Publications

CONFERENCE 2024 SAE WCX 2024 PRIMARY AUTHOR

Automotive Intermediate Shaft Design & Bearing Selection for a Propulsion Switched Reluctance Motor in a Battery Electric Vehicle

Designed and optimized an intermediate shaft to distribute SRM torque equally between both rear CV axles. Equal-length axles were required to eliminate torque steer under hard acceleration. Optimization covered material selection, inner/outer diameter, and torsional stiffness — maximizing shaft rigidity while minimizing mass.

Shaft Design Bearing Selection Structural Optimization Torque Distribution BEV Driveline
CONFERENCE 2025 ITEC 2025

Evaluation of Powertrain Architectures for McMaster EcoCAR EV Considering Traditional Electric Machines and Switched Reluctance Machines

Assessed trade-offs between the EcoCAR's dual-motor architecture — AAM induction motor (front) and SRM (rear) — against alternatives such as a dual PM configuration. Primary contribution covered the mechanical packaging challenges of each architecture and their impact on performance target feasibility.

Powertrain Architecture SRM Mechanical Packaging EcoCAR
JOURNAL 2026 IEEE TTE

Design and Integration of a Traction Inverter for a 150-kW Switched Reluctance Motor (SRM) Drive Unit in a Cadillac LYRIQ

Performed thermal validation in ANSYS Fluent to verify the Foster network model for the traction inverter's power switches. Compared simulated thermal impedance behaviour against experimental results to confirm model accuracy under real drive conditions.

ANSYS Fluent Thermal Validation Foster Network Power Electronics SRM
